A separate pool still in development.

Tidepool explores a directed-yield pool connected to Turntide Islands. It would have its own membership, governance, and financial operations. Joining the Turntide community does not make someone a Tidepool member.

Public operation has not begun.

The economic and governance specifications remain drafts for owner review. The published parameter record lists no admitted member enterprises and no closed cycles. This site offers no admission request, wallet connection, transfer, or yield routing.

What the proposal needs

Approved operating terms, admitted members, a demonstrated financial and governance path, and recorded cycle outcomes would be needed before this page could describe an operating pool.

What exists now

Draft economics and governance specifications dated 5 September 2026, plus a parameter snapshot with empty roster and cycle lists. An address or draft rule alone does not establish an operating service.

Turntide Islands is open for climate adaptation discussion and knowledge work. Its community path is separate from any future Tidepool admission.
